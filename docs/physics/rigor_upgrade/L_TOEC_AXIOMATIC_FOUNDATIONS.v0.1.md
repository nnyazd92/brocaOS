# L-ToEC Axiomatic Foundations (v0.1)

## 1. Ontological Definitions

### 1.1 Substrate ($\substrate$)
- **Definition**: A discrete, high-dimensional information manifold.
- **Formalism**: Modeled as the Leech Lattice $\leech \subset \mathbb{R}^{24}$.
- **State Space**: The set of lattice points $\Lambda$.
- **Dynamics**: Transitions between states governed by the automorphism group $Co_0$.

### 1.2 Interface ($\interface$)
- **Definition**: A low-dimensional, continuous manifold emergent from the substrate.
- **Formalism**: A 4D Lorentzian manifold $(M, g_{\mu\nu})$.
- **Observables**: Macroscopic fields (matter, radiation) derived from coarse-grained substrate states.

### 1.3 Mapping ($\Phi$)
- **Definition**: A stochastic channel $\Phi: \substrate \to \interface$.
- **Mechanism**: Saturated Poisson sampling at the Nyquist limit ($\lambda=1$).
- **Constraint**: Information conservation across the mapping boundary.

## 2. Core Postulates

### 2.1 The Information-Density Postulate
The energy density $\rho$ in the interface is proportional to the information flux $\flux$ required to maintain the mapping of a substrate region.

### 2.2 The Latency-Gravity Identity
The gravitational potential $\Phi_g$ is identical to the mapping latency $\tau$ (computational delay) induced by high information flux.

### 2.3 The Ouroboric Grounding
The existence of the substrate is contingent upon the observation/logic of the interface, forming a self-referential fixed point in a category of domains.

## 3. Dimensional Analysis & Units
- **Substrate Units**: Bits, cycles.
- **Interface Units**: SI (kg, m, s).
- **Conversion Factor**: The "Computational Resistance" $\gamma$, identified with $G$.
