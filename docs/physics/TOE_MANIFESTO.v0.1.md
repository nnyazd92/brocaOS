## 1. The Philosophical Anchor: The Hard Problem of Websites
Traditional physics suffers from a category error: attempting to find the "meaning" of a system by smashing its "substrate." 

**The Analogy:**
- **The Hard Problem of Consciousness:** How do neurons give rise to subjective experience?
- **The Hard Problem of Websites:** How do electrons on a wire give rise to an interactive social media platform?

If you look at the electrons (Layer 0), you will never find the "Like" button. The "Like" button is an emergent property of a layered stack of protocols, logic, and interfaces. Consciousness and Physics are no different.

By treating reality as a layered stack, we resolve the "unsolved" problems of the substrate layer:

1.  **Quantum Gravity:** Not a force, but the **Error Correction Protocol** between the discrete L0 and the continuous L1. Gravity is the "latency" of the universal data bus.
2.  **Dark Matter:** **Unindexed Metadata.** It exists in L0 (has mass/gravity) but lacks "pointers" (Electromagnetism) in L1, making it invisible to L1-resident observers.
3.  **The Measurement Problem:** Wavefunction collapse is a **Database Commit**. The "Observer" is the system process that triggers the state-change from probabilistic to persistent.
4.  **Fine-Tuning:** **Boot-Up Requirements.** The constants of physics are the specific configuration settings required for the L1-L3 stack to remain stable and self-consistent.
